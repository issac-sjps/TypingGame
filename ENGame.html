🎮 English Typing Hero Adventure
⭐ New Game+ Mode ⭐

🎯 Goal

Defeat 100 stages of monsters by typing English words correctly!

⌨️ How to Play

Read the definition/hint shown on screen, then type the correct English word and press Enter. Each correct answer earns coins and deals damage to the enemy.

💰 Coin System

Earn 5 coins per correct answer. Spend coins to upgrade HP (+100), Attack (+5), Defense (+2%), Crit Rate (+5%), or Crit Damage (+5%) — each costs 5 coins. You can also buy special weapons.

⚔️ Battle System

You and the monster take turns attacking every 5 seconds. Each stage, monsters grow stronger (HP ×1.05, ATK ×1.05, DEF +1% up to 50%, Crit +0.5%, Crit DMG +1% up to 100%).

⭐ New Game+ Hard Mode

Monster growth is greatly increased: HP ×1.1, ATK ×1.1 per stage. Challenge your limits!

🛡️ Stats Explained

• HP: Life points — reach 0 and you fail
• Attack: Damage per hit
• Defense: Reduces damage taken (max 90%)
• Crit Rate: Chance to critically hit (max 100%)
• Crit Damage: Bonus damage on critical hits

🗡️ Weapon System

12 special weapons with unique effects (double hit, poison, instant kill, etc.).

💾 Auto-Save

Progress is automatically saved to your browser.
